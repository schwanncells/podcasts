# Unchained — "Why Pandl Calls Gold and Bitcoin Buys Here"

**Host:** Steve Ehrlich (head of research, Sharplink). **Guest:** Zach Pandl (head of research, Grayscale; former senior economist, Goldman Sachs).

**Gold and Bitcoin Through the Iran War**

Pandl argues the Iran war performance of both assets was driven by short-term speculation rather than any change in their fundamental cases. Bitcoin rose early — an oversold reversion — then struggled. Gold finished its worst quarter in years. Neither move, in his view, reflects a shift in the underlying thesis. He points to World Gold Council data showing central banks repatriating gold from London and New York as evidence that demand for secure stores of value remains intact, and notes that Bitcoin's transferability gives it a structural advantage over gold in a world where physical custody increasingly matters.

**The Case for Buying Here**

Pandl traces gold's mega-trend to 2022: Russia's invasion of Ukraine prompted the U.S. and allies to sanction Russia's central bank reserves, a moment that fundamentally altered how sovereigns think about holding assets in foreign custody. That demand pushed gold from roughly $2,000 to $4,000. The subsequent move to $5,500 was retail return-chasing — speculative excess that is now rotating into chips and semiconductors, perpetual futures, and pre-IPO trades. Pandl frames this washout as the entry point: "Both gold and Bitcoin, to me, have very strong fundamental foundations. And that has to do with this deficit and debt imbalance that's not going to go anywhere for a long period of time." He says both assets "look pretty good here" for investors willing to look past the near-term noise.

**DeFi Revenue as the H2 Thesis**

For the second half of the year, Pandl shifts focus to specific DeFi assets. Regulatory progress — the Clarity Act and stablecoin legislation — has given protocol teams room to structure businesses and discuss revenue openly for the first time. Pandl names Hyperliquid as the clear benchmark: it produces real revenue and returns it to token holders, demonstrating what a legitimate on-chain business model looks like. Grayscale has published a DCF-based price target on Aave, and Uniswap received a buy rating from Standard Chartered. Pandl sees this combination of regulatory clarity and demonstrated earnings power as the specific catalyst for DeFi assets in H2.
